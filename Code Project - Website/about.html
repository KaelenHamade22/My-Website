ABOUT

A quick marketing look at the Nike Mind 001.

The four Ps

Product

A sensory mule with 22 foam nodes.

Price

From $95. The Mind 002 sneaker is $145.

Place

Nike.com and some retail partners.

Promotion

"Think less. Feel more." plus athlete and wellness stories.

Who is it for?

Athletes who want a pre-game or recovery routine, and everyday people who want comfy, calm-looking shoes. Most Nike shoes sell on speed or cushioning, but the Mind 001 sells on how it makes you feel.
