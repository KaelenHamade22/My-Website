Not just another shoe

The Nike Mind 001 came out in January 2026 and starts at $95. Nike calls it its first neuroscience-based footwear.

Instead of being built for speed, it is built to be felt. The nodes on the sole move as you walk, which is meant to make you more aware of your body and what is around you.
