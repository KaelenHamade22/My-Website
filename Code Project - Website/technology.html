What makes the Mind 001 different from a normal shoe.

How it works

Each Mind 001 has 22 separate foam nodes on the bottom. They move on their own as you step, so your foot feels lots of small changes in pressure instead of one flat surface.

Nike says this extra feeling under the foot helps you pay attention to your body and surroundings, so you feel calm, focused and present.
